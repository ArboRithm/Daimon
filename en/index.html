What Daimon is ​

AI assistants reason well but see nothing of your desktop. Daimon is the missing organ: a small resident application that exposes perception (the screen, the structure of windows) and action (mouse, keyboard, windows) to any AI client that speaks the Model Context Protocol.

Daimon is a tool, not an assistant. It answers when an AI client queries it, never the other way round: it calls no AI model itself.

Where to start ​

  1. Install Daimon on macOS or Windows.
  2. Grant the permissions (macOS) and register your AI clients.
  3. Understand the mental model: Vue, Touché, Mains, Overlay, Face.
  4. Set the Mains ceiling to your liking.
  5. When in doubt, ask your AI to call daimon_doctor: see troubleshooting.
